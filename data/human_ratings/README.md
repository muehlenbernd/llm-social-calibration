# Human Ratings Data

The human behavioral data used as benchmark in this study originates from:

> Solt, S., Mühlenbernd, R., & Burbelko, M. (2025). Social meaning and pragmatic reasoning:
> The case of (im)precision. In *Proceedings of the Experiments in Linguistic Meaning (ELM 3)*,
> pp. 371–382. Linguistic Society of America.

The data are not redistributed here. Please contact the authors for access,
or refer to the original publication.

The expected filename is `impX1.csv`. Place it in this directory before running
`src/analysis/main_metrics.py` or `src/analysis/main_correl.py`.
